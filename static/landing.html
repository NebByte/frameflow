Which shots can we earn?

Converting a film to a 270° format takes about two months per title, and most of that goes on a question that isn’t an art problem: which shots are even possible. A panning camera already photographed the side walls. A locked-off close-up never did.

Frameflow answers that in seconds per shot, recovers the walls it can from the film’s own footage, and never counts an invented pixel as a photographed one.

“Is locked_off.mp4 worth converting?” A camera that never moved. Watch it refuse, and say why. “Triage pan_flat.mp4 — which shots are worth an artist week?” Per-shot verdicts, geometry in dB, coverage as a floor. “Convert it, then settle the walls.” Three projector feeds, plus what the settle pass fixed for free.
